--- OPEN B. WHY does edge typing hurt?  NEW, and now the most valuable open question. ----------
The n=7 family says WHICH component costs the graph its benefit but not why. Arms on the frozen fold:
untyped +0.0904 > expression_only +0.0861 > condition_gated +0.0818 > typed_static +0.0740. Typing is
worth -0.0120 (7/7 seeds, survives both corrections); the gate repairs only part of that and not
significantly (+0.0077, Bonferroni 0.131).
CANDIDATE MECHANISMS, none isolated:
  (i)   per-relation normalisation — already MEASURED to depress the mean gate tenfold (0.03 and 0.12
        against 0.73) in the arch search. Strongest suspect: it may be the epoch-0 gate-collapse
        failure arriving through a second door.
  (ii)  the condition gate's initialisation.
  (iii) the decoder mixture gamma.
  (iv)  relation-specific parameter count: typed message passing has ~3x the parameters over the same
        edges, so this may be capacity/overfitting rather than anything about types.
CHEAPEST DISCRIMINATING TEST: an arm with typed relations but SHARED weights across relation types.
If it behaves like untyped, the damage is parameter count (iv). If it behaves like typed_static, the
damage is the relation partition itself. ~6-7 GPU-h per seed on the reference fold, 4 seeds.
WHY IT MATTERS: the paper currently says our encoder discards the signal. Naming the mechanism would
turn a diagnosis into a prescription, which is what this venue's rubric rewards most.

--- OPEN C. L2. The detection floor. -------------------------- ~60-100 GPU-h. Highest remaining. ---
PAPER SAYS: the CI upper limit is per fold; the MDE from 5 seeds is itself uncertain (0.005 to 0.025).
CLOSE WITH (a) AN EMPIRICAL FLOOR. Inject a known graph-dependent component into the real DE matrix at
a ladder of effect sizes: for each target add delta * (mean response of its k-hop PPI neighbours,
computed from TRAIN-FOLD RESPONSES ONLY) for delta in {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40} response SDs.
Re-run condition_gated vs expression_only at n>=4 per rung. The smallest delta that reliably clears
both corrections is the pipeline's MEASURED sensitivity.
  LEAKAGE IS THE WHOLE RISK: if the injected signal touches val responses the control is worthless.
  Write that check as a test and WATCH IT FAIL before trusting it.
  A HIGH floor is also a finding — a limitation with a number instead of a hedge.
CLOSE WITH (b) SIMULATION POWER over the variance structure now measured at THREE levels: seed
(no-graph 0.0003-0.0027; graph arms 6-32x that), re-draw, and dataset. How many seeds, re-draws and
datasets are needed to detect the benefit graph-prior papers report? If it exceeds what anyone runs,
that is the paper's most quotable sentence. CPU only; use the statistical-power skill.
NOW ALSO CALIBRATES OPEN B: the untyped arm's real +0.0043 gives the ladder a known true-positive to
sit against, which it did not have when this item was written.
CLOSED WHEN: the paper states the null in units of a measured floor rather than a modelled MDE.
